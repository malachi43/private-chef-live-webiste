Help centre

Questions, answered.

When does Private Chef launch?

We're getting ready to launch in Lagos. Join the waitlist and we'll email you first when bookings open.

What's the difference between Chef at Home and Cook & Deliver?

With Chef at Home, the chef comes to your home, cooks in your kitchen and serves. With Cook & Deliver, the chef cooks in their own kitchen and delivers the meal to your door.

How far ahead should I book?

Most chefs accept bookings up to 30 days ahead. For dinners, two or three days is usually enough; for parties of 20 or more, book at least a week ahead.

Do I need to buy the ingredients?

No. The chef shops for fresh ingredients and the cost is included in the price you see. If you would rather supply them yourself, you can say so in your request.

Can the chef cook for allergies or diets?

Yes. Add allergies and dietary needs when you book. Chefs confirm what they can do before they accept, so nothing is left to chance on the day.

Which areas do you cover?

We're starting across Lagos: Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Ajah, Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja and Gbagada. Chefs choose how far they travel, so you only see chefs who can reach you.

What does it cost to join as a chef?

Your first 30 days are free on every plan. After that, you pick a monthly plan that fits your business, from ₦5,000 a month.

What do I need to get verified?

A government ID (NIN slip, passport or driver's licence), a food handler certificate or proof of food safety training, your bank details for payouts and, if you have one, your CAC registration.

Who sets the prices?

You do. Price per guest, per dish or by custom quote. You can change your prices at any time; confirmed bookings keep the price agreed.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are sent to your bank account within 24 hours of a completed booking.

Can I turn down a booking?

Yes. You choose which requests to accept. If you decline, the client gets an automatic refund and nothing counts against you.

How does payment work?

You pay securely when you book and we hold the money. The chef is paid only after your meal is done.

What fees will I pay?

You see the full price before you pay: the food, any travel fee and a service fee. There is nothing extra to pay on the day.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Every booking shows its cancellation policy before you pay, so you know exactly what's refundable and when.

Which payment methods can I use?

Debit and credit cards, bank transfer and USSD. Payments are processed by a licensed Nigerian payment provider.

How are chefs verified?

Every chef shares a government ID and their food safety and business documents. Our team reviews them before a profile goes live, and verified chefs carry a green seal.

Who sees my address?

Chefs see only your area while they decide. Your exact address is shared once the booking is confirmed.

What if something goes wrong?

Report it in the app or message support. We hold the payment while we look into it, and we reply within 2 hours in support hours.

Are reviews real?

Only clients with a completed booking can leave a review, so every rating comes from a real meal.

Four steps

From craving to clean plates.

  1. 01

    Find your chef

    Search by dish, date and number of guests. Every chef shows their menus, prices, reviews and the green verified seal.

  2. 02

    Book in a few taps

    Pick a menu, tell the chef about allergies and your kitchen, and see the full price: food, travel and service fee.

  3. 03

    Pay, and we hold it

    You pay by card, transfer or USSD. We hold the money. Your exact address is shared once the chef confirms.

  4. 04

    Enjoy, then rate

    The chef cooks, serves and cleans up. When you're happy, the payment is released and you leave a review.

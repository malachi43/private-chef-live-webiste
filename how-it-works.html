How it works

Booking a chef, start to finish.

Four steps from craving to clean plates. You see the full price before you pay, and the chef is paid only after your meal.

  1. 1Find
  2. 2Book
  3. 3Pay
  4. 4Enjoy
Four steps

From craving to clean plates.

  1. 01

    Find your chef

    Search by dish, date and number of guests. Every chef shows their menus, prices, reviews and the green verified seal.

  2. 02

    Book in a few taps

    Pick a menu, tell the chef about allergies and your kitchen, and see the full price: food, travel and service fee.

  3. 03

    Pay, and we hold it

    You pay by card, transfer or USSD. We hold the money. Your exact address is shared once the chef confirms.

  4. 04

    Enjoy, then rate

    The chef cooks, serves and cleans up. When you're happy, the payment is released and you leave a review.

What's the difference between Chef at Home and Cook & Deliver?

With Chef at Home, the chef comes to your home, cooks in your kitchen and serves. With Cook & Deliver, the chef cooks in their own kitchen and delivers the meal to your door.

How does payment work?

You pay securely when you book and we hold the money. The chef is paid only after your meal is done.

What fees will I pay?

You see the full price before you pay: the food, any travel fee and a service fee. There is nothing extra to pay on the day.

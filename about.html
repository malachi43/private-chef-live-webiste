About Private Chef

Great cooks deserve great tables. Lagos deserves both.

Our story

Why we started.

  1. Chapter one

    Lagos is full of brilliant cooks

    Home cooks, caterers and trained chefs make food people talk about for weeks. Most of them find work by word of mouth, one WhatsApp message at a time.

  2. Chapter two

    Booking them was hard

    Finding someone you can trust in your home, agreeing a price and paying a stranger upfront all felt risky. So most people didn't, and most cooks stayed hidden.

  3. Chapter three

    So we built the safe way to do it

    Verified chefs, clear prices and payment held until the meal is done. Clients book with confidence, and chefs get paid properly for their craft.

What we stand for

Four promises, every booking.

  • Trust first

    Every chef is checked before they cook for anyone.

  • Fair to chefs

    Chefs set their prices and get paid within a day.

  • Honest prices

    The price you see is the price you pay, fees included.

  • Proudly Lagos

    Built here, for the way Lagos eats and celebrates.

Lagos first

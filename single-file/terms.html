1. About these terms

These terms cover your use of the Private Chef website and app, run by C-ZUCHI GLOBAL SERVICES LIMITED in Lagos, Nigeria. By creating an account or making a booking, you agree to them.

2. Who we are

Private Chef is a marketplace. We connect clients with independent chefs and handle booking and payment. Chefs are not our employees; each chef is responsible for the food they cook and the service they give.

3. Your account

You must be 18 or older and give accurate details. Keep your login safe; you are responsible for what happens in your account.

4. Bookings

A booking is confirmed when the chef accepts your request and your payment succeeds. The booking page shows the menu, date, guests, total price and cancellation policy before you pay.

5. Payments and fees

You pay the full price when you book. We hold the payment and release it to the chef after the booking is complete. Prices include food, any travel fee and our service fee.

6. Cancellations and refunds

If a chef declines or cancels, you receive a full refund. If you cancel, the refund follows the policy shown on your booking. Refunds return to your original payment method within 5 to 10 working days.

7. Chefs

Chefs must pass verification, keep their documents current and follow food safety law. Chef plans are billed monthly after a 30-day free period and can be cancelled at any time.

8. Reviews and conduct

Reviews must be honest and based on a real booking. We remove content that is abusive, false or unlawful, and may suspend accounts that break these terms.

9. Liability

We work to keep the platform safe and available, but we are not liable for losses caused by a chef or client beyond what the law allows. Nothing here limits rights you have under Nigerian consumer law.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will tell you about important changes before they apply. Questions: hello@privatechef.ng.

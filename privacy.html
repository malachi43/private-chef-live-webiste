1. Our approach

We collect only what we need to run bookings safely, and we never sell your personal data. This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What we collect

Your name, phone number, email and area; your address once you book; booking and payment records; messages with chefs; and, for chefs, identity and verification documents.

3. How we use it

To create your account, match you with chefs, process payments, keep the platform safe, provide support and, if you agree, send you news about Private Chef.

4. Who we share it with

Your chef sees your area while deciding and your exact address once a booking is confirmed. Payment providers process payments. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open. Booking and payment records are kept for 6 years for tax and legal reasons. Waitlist details are deleted if we don't launch in your area within 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Email hello@privatechef.ng and we will reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Payment card details are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.

8. Cookies

The website uses essential cookies to work and, with your consent, analytics cookies to improve it.

9. Contact

Data protection questions: hello@privatechef.ng. Private Chef, C-ZUCHI GLOBAL SERVICES LIMITED, Lagos, Nigeria.
